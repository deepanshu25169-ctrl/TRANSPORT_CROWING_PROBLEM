## Held-out segment error (recorded passengers per segment)

| Model | n segment observations | MAE | RMSE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Pattern + day type + time bin (14-day window) | 2,316,648 | **0.482** | **1.024** |
| Baseline: typical route load, no time of day | 2,316,648 | **0.463** | 1.058 |

The time-aware model reduces large errors (RMSE) but is slightly worse on MAE than the
route-only baseline. This is a genuine negative result: with sparse tickets per run, fine
bins add variance. The baseline remains the safer point estimate for MAE; the time-aware
model is useful for identifying busy stretches and periods.

## What the ticket data supports

- Relative recorded demand by route pattern, direction, day type and segment.
- Comparison of scheduled alternatives on identical stretches and in identical windows.
- Identification of uncertainty: coverage gaps, ambiguous names, unknown vehicle models.

## What it does not support

- Total onboard passengers (ticket coverage q is unknown; no citywide multiplier applied).
- Physical seat availability, standing comfort or a cross-route physical comfort ranking.
- Whether an unobserved scheduled departure actually operated.
